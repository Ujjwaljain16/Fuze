# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This project welcomes contributors of every background and level of experience. In its spaces
(issues, pull requests, discussions) we expect people to:

- be respectful and assume good faith;
- give and accept constructive, specific feedback;
- focus on what is best for the project and its users;
- keep discussion on the technical merits.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language or imagery,
publishing others' private information, trolling, and sustained disruption of discussion.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and contributions that do not follow this
code, and may restrict participation for repeated or serious violations. To report a problem, use
GitHub's "Report content" feature on the item, or contact the maintainer through the profile linked
in the README. Reports are handled privately and in good faith.

This code applies within project spaces and when someone is representing the project in public.
It is adapted from the ideas of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
